3 · Sets: playlists with parts, built to mix

A set is a playlist split into parts (sub-playlists). Each track shows its tempo, key and detected genre, and each gap shows how the two tracks will mix. The curve on top shows tempo and energy across the whole set. Auto-order and fill tools work on one part or on the whole set.

  • Genre detection runs with the tempo analysis. Confident genres show as solid tags; doubtful ones are dashed with a percentage, and one click confirms or fixes them.
  • Auto-order picks among: smooth tempo, harmonic (key wheel), energy arc. It moves tracks only inside a part, unless you ask for the whole set.
  • Fill from library asks for a genre, a tempo range and a length, and proposes tracks from any library, online ones included.
