Where everything goes

  • Playback: language, quality, play on open, AI frame rates (toggle, anime hold, engine status + setup)
  • Subtitles: unchanged controls, preview pinned while scrolling
  • Sources & search: sites on/off, preferred source, one card per show, TMDB key, link to YouTube & Twitch
  • Downloads: destination, folder, try-first order, delete toggle (moved out of Danger zone)
  • Music: libraries, genre model, YouTube Music cookies, share tokens
  • Notifications: WhatsApp toggle, number, CallMeBot key
  • Server: status, log, restart / stop, Nightly, the Node.js warning. Share: the two QR codes
  • "Danger zone" goes away: each risky switch lives next to what it affects, in red.
