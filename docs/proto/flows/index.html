Multiverse

Every future of the flow. The lit path is the one the mix follows; click a chunk to route through it.

Presets

Drop a chunk (solid) on the header row to insert it there. Drop a layer (dashed) on a lane to replace that layer of the chunk under it.

Score

Curves: drag a point; double-click to add one; right-click a point to remove it (the last two remove the layer); double-click an empty lane to add it. Harmony: click the lane to place a modulation at that point (every other join is a match); click an event to choose its kind, drag it, right-click to remove it; right-click the lane to free the chunk of harmony. Genre: click to pick, shift-click for must / prefer, double-click to split, right-click to merge. Header: drag a boundary to resize; click a chunk name for Branch here / Delete; pills at a fork pick the future (auto = generator). Mix: click a track to play from it.
