An empty playlist in the editor

"New playlist" now opens the editor on an empty playlist. Today the middle column says "Empty. Add tracks…" in small grey text, and dropping there only works on that one line. These options make the first move obvious: drag on desktop, tap on phones.

  • STORY 1I just made a playlist. Without reading anything, I can see how the first track gets in.
  • STORY 2I'm dragging a track. Before I let go, I know where it lands and that it will work.
  • STORY 3On my phone I can't drag. I can still add the first track in one or two taps.

ADrop well

The whole middle column becomes a large dashed area with one instruction: drag tracks here. It lights up while you drag over it, and anywhere inside counts. It's the simplest option and the most familiar pattern (an upload box).

CShow the move once

A short card next to the first track explains the move, with a dashed path to the landing spot. It shows only on your first empty playlist and goes away for good with "Got it" or your first drop. After that, the empty state is option A's well, smaller.
