Blend playlists: one object for mixes, playlists and the set

From your answers: a mix is a playlist in blend mode · Blend orders it, your pins win · the order changes as you edit and locks when you press Play · a flow can add tracks and shape the order · built in the board · Your set and the Mixes list go away. The set's machinery (pins, Blend by, energy line, join problems + Fix) moves onto the open playlist.

Stories

  1. Start one. "New playlist" in the board opens it in the centre, empty, blend on. Tape and explorer stay for picking.
  2. Add a track. Drag or + from the tape/explorer: it lands where it blends best; dropped between two rows, it's pinned there.
  3. Pin / move. Drag a row → pinned at its new place; the rest re-orders; moved rows marked; Undo.
  4. Fill from a flow. Pick a flow + minutes: it adds tracks that fit, around your pins.
  5. Shape the order. Hold / Lift / Ease / Arc, or a flow's energy curve.
  6. Fix a weak join. Existing Fix panel: bridge, move, or keep.
  7. Play. What you see is what plays: the order locks. Editing while it plays re-blends only what's after the playing track.
  8. Hand order. Turn blend off: the current order freezes and stays yours (old playlists open like this).
  9. On the couch. Phone: open, tap a row to swap/pin/move, hold to drag, Play in thumb reach.

Questions for this round

  1. Drop without a target: a track dropped on the list (not between two rows) lands where it blends best, unpinned. OK, or always pinned where it's dropped?
  2. Lock on Play: an edit while playing re-blends only after the playing track (D3/P4). OK?
  3. Old hand playlists open in Hand order; switching one to Blend reorders the whole list (Undo available). OK?
  4. The queue: playing a blend playlist replaces what's after the playing track, as Play the set does today. Should the queue show the playlist as one block or as its tracks?
