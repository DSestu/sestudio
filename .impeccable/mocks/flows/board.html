Flows · one board, five surfaces

Each surface on the app's chrome, desktop (1024 wide) and phone (390). Low fidelity on purpose: layout, hierarchy and the words; the look follows the design system. Yellow tags mark what to decide.

1 · Entry: the Music tab opens on the dial, flows under it, the library behind

Three sliders, then Play: a flow is generated around them and plays. Below, your flows: recent first, then presets. Albums / Artists / Tracks move behind a "Library" tab. The pull is the fourth control, folded by default.
